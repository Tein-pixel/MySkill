# Android Wearos Compose

## Workflow

Confirm Wear OS version, target devices, Compose/Material versions, and interaction model. Design for circular viewports and short glanceable tasks. Use current Wear Compose Material APIs and recommended scaffold/layout patterns; verify exact APIs against official docs. Support rotary input, swipes, hardware buttons, ambient mode, and complication or tile boundaries where relevant. Minimize network and wakeups, keep interactions concise, and handle phone-watch data synchronization explicitly. Test on round and square emulators or devices, different screen sizes, ambient transitions, and low-power conditions.

## Completion checks
- State what was inspected and what remains unknown.
- Keep the work within the user's scope and authorization.
- Preserve existing behavior and project conventions.
- Verify important changes with an observable check.
- Report evidence, caveats, and unverified areas separately.
