# Android Play Policy Review

## Workflow

Identify the app category, target audience, data collected/shared, SDKs, ads, account creation/deletion, permissions, and monetization. Read current official Google Play policy and developer documentation; policies and deadlines change, so record the research date and relevant policy sections. Trace each declaration to actual app behavior and third-party SDK behavior. Flag mismatches, missing disclosures, excessive permissions, incomplete deletion flows, or unclear consent. Do not promise store approval or invent policy interpretations. Separate confirmed issues from items requiring developer-console or legal review. Produce a prioritized remediation checklist and evidence links.

## Completion checks
- State what was inspected and what remains unknown.
- Keep the work within the user's scope and authorization.
- Preserve existing behavior and project conventions.
- Verify important changes with an observable check.
- Report evidence, caveats, and unverified areas separately.
