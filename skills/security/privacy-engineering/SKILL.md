# Privacy Engineering

## Workflow

Map personal data, purposes, collection points, recipients, storage, transfers, retention, and user controls. Minimize collection and access; define lawful basis and consent requirements with qualified reviewers where relevant. Use privacy-by-design defaults, access controls, encryption, deletion workflows, auditability, and data minimization in telemetry. Check current jurisdiction-specific laws and platform policies rather than making universal legal claims. Avoid placing sensitive data in logs, analytics, prompts, or training datasets without approved controls. Produce a data-flow inventory and prioritized privacy risks.

## Completion checks
- State what was inspected and what remains unknown.
- Keep the work within the user's scope and authorization.
- Preserve existing behavior and project conventions.
- Verify important changes with an observable check.
- Report evidence, caveats, and unverified areas separately.
