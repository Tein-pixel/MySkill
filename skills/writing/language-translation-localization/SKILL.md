# Language Translation Localization

## Workflow

Identify source and target languages, locale, audience, domain, desired tone, and whether literal or transcreated output is needed. Preserve names, numbers, units, placeholders, variables, links, and formatting. Build a glossary for recurring terms and check consistency. Flag ambiguity instead of guessing. Do not translate code identifiers or protected brand terms unless requested. Review idiom, register, cultural references, and accessibility. Distinguish translation from fact-checking; do not silently alter factual claims.

## Completion checks
- State what was inspected and what remains unknown.
- Keep the work within the user's scope and authorization.
- Preserve existing behavior and project conventions.
- Verify important changes with an observable check.
- Report evidence, caveats, and unverified areas separately.
