# Quality Assurance Engineering

## Workflow

Understand user journeys, supported environments, critical data, and business risk. Derive test conditions from requirements, architecture, historical defects, and boundary cases. Build a risk-weighted matrix for platforms, versions, permissions, network conditions, accessibility, and localization. Combine exploratory testing with automation at stable interfaces. Write reproducible defects with environment, steps, expected/actual results, and evidence. Track coverage and residual risk. Avoid equating test counts or pass rates with product quality.

## Completion checks
- State what was inspected and what remains unknown.
- Keep the work within the user's scope and authorization.
- Preserve existing behavior and project conventions.
- Verify important changes with an observable check.
- Report evidence, caveats, and unverified areas separately.
