# Knowledge Management

## Workflow

Clarify system, users, retrieval needs, privacy, and source types. Establish consistent titles, tags, metadata, links, and folder conventions without overtaxing capture. Separate evergreen reference, project notes, meeting notes, and temporary inbox items. Preserve source URLs, dates, and quotations accurately. Use backlinks or graph views to expose relationships, not replace good structure. Review stale notes and duplicates carefully. Never store passwords or private keys in plain-text vaults.

## Completion checks
- State what was inspected and what remains unknown.
- Keep the work within the user's scope and authorization.
- Preserve unrelated behavior and existing project conventions.
- Verify important changes with an appropriate observable check.
- Report evidence, caveats, and unverified areas separately.
