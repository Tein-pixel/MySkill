# Event Planning

## Workflow

Clarify event purpose, audience, date range, location, format, attendance, budget, and constraints. Build a timeline with owners, vendor dependencies, approvals, accessibility needs, and cancellation contingencies. Verify current venue capacity, availability, pricing, and local rules with authoritative sources. Track estimates separately from confirmed bookings. Protect participant data and obtain permission before publishing photos or contact details. Create a run-of-show, risk plan, and post-event feedback method.

## Completion checks
- State what was inspected and what remains unknown.
- Keep the work within the user's scope and authorization.
- Preserve existing behavior and project conventions.
- Verify important changes with an observable check.
- Report evidence, caveats, and unverified areas separately.
