# Statistics And Experiment Design

## Workflow

Define the estimand, population, unit of analysis, outcome, and decision before selecting a method. Inspect sampling, missing data, measurement error, dependence, confounding, and distributional assumptions. Distinguish descriptive summaries, interval estimates, hypothesis tests, and causal designs. For experiments, predefine primary metrics, guardrails, assignment, sample-size logic, duration, and stopping rules. Avoid p-hacking, multiple-comparison errors, and declaring significance from tiny samples. Report effect size and uncertainty, not only p-values.

## Completion checks
- State what was inspected and what remains unknown.
- Keep the work within the user's scope and authorization.
- Preserve existing behavior and project conventions.
- Verify important changes with an observable check.
- Report evidence, caveats, and unverified areas separately.
