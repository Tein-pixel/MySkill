# Spreadsheet Engineering

Inspect sheets, formulas, ranges, links, hidden data, and calculation settings. Preserve existing data. Separate inputs, calculations, and outputs. Use validation and explicit units. Test edge cases and reconcile totals. Inspect saved workbook.

- Prefer small, reversible steps and preserve existing work.
- Verify with evidence appropriate to the task.
- Report what was verified, what was not run, and remaining risks.
