# Machine Learning Engineering

## Workflow

Clarify task, labels, deployment constraints, failure costs, and baseline. Inspect data provenance, consent, leakage, representativeness, and splits before training. Establish a simple baseline and reproducible environment. Track code, data, model, configuration, metrics, and random seeds. Evaluate on suitable held-out data and stress-test relevant subgroups and edge cases. Monitor drift and performance after deployment. Consider latency, cost, energy, privacy, and rollback. Do not claim model quality from training accuracy alone.

## Completion checks
- State what was inspected and what remains unknown.
- Keep the work within the user's scope and authorization.
- Preserve existing behavior and project conventions.
- Verify important changes with an observable check.
- Report evidence, caveats, and unverified areas separately.
