# Motion Design

## Workflow

Identify what motion communicates: hierarchy, continuity, feedback, spatial relationships, or state change. Use duration and easing appropriate to distance, input, and urgency; do not animate every element by default. For direct-manipulation gestures, keep response coupled to pointer/finger movement and handle interruption naturally. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Check frame pacing, layout shifts, battery impact, and accessibility. Remove animations that delay or distract.

## Completion checks
- State what was inspected and what remains unknown.
- Keep the work within the user's scope and authorization.
- Preserve unrelated behavior and existing project conventions.
- Verify important changes with an appropriate observable check.
- Report evidence, caveats, and unverified areas separately.
