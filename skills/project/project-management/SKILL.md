# Project Management

Define objectives, deliverables, boundaries, stakeholders, and constraints. Decompose into milestones with dependencies and acceptance criteria. Maintain risks and decisions. Track evidence-based progress. Surface blockers early and record change impact. Close with acceptance and handover.

## Completion checks
- Confirm the task matches this skill's scope.
- Inspect relevant source, project, and user constraints.
- State assumptions and unknowns instead of inventing facts.
- Prefer small, reversible steps and preserve existing work.
- Verify with evidence appropriate to the task.
- Report what was verified, what was not run, and remaining risks.
