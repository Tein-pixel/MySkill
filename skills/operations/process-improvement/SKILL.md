# Process Improvement

## Workflow

Map the current workflow from trigger to outcome, including roles, handoffs, systems, queues, and exceptions. Gather cycle-time, rework, error, and volume data when available. Distinguish symptoms from root causes. Identify bottlenecks, duplicated work, unclear ownership, and control gaps. Design the smallest safe pilot and define metrics, rollback, training, and stakeholder impact. Do not automate a broken process before clarifying its rules.

## Completion checks
- State what was inspected and what remains unknown.
- Keep the work within the user's scope and authorization.
- Preserve unrelated behavior and existing project conventions.
- Verify important changes with an appropriate observable check.
- Report evidence, caveats, and unverified areas separately.
