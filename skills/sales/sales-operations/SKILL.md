# Sales Operations

## Workflow

Clarify product, audience, approved channels, consent rules, and sales process. Define qualification criteria and record provenance. Design pipeline stages with exit conditions, next actions, and ownership. Draft respectful follow-ups and honor opt-outs. Do not scrape private contact details, impersonate people, spam, or misrepresent claims. Measure conversion, cycle time, retention, and fit rather than message volume.

## Completion checks
- State what was inspected and what remains unknown.
- Keep the work within the user's scope and authorization.
- Preserve unrelated behavior and existing project conventions.
- Verify important changes with an appropriate observable check.
- Report evidence, caveats, and unverified areas separately.
