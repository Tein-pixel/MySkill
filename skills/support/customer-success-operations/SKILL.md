# Customer Success Operations

## Workflow

Clarify customer goals, product use case, lifecycle stage, service commitments, and escalation routes. Build onboarding around measurable first-value milestones. Monitor adoption and feedback with consent and role-appropriate access. Distinguish product defects from training gaps or expectation mismatch. Document commitments accurately and coordinate with support or product teams. Avoid manipulative retention tactics, false promises, or unauthorized access to customer accounts. Measure customer outcomes rather than activity volume.

## Completion checks
- State what was inspected and what remains unknown.
- Keep the work within the user's scope and authorization.
- Preserve existing behavior and project conventions.
- Verify important changes with an observable check.
- Report evidence, caveats, and unverified areas separately.
