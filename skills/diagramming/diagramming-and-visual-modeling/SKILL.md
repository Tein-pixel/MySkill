# Diagramming And Visual Modeling

## Workflow

Identify the question the diagram should answer and its reader. Choose the simplest useful notation such as Mermaid flowcharts, sequence diagrams, state diagrams, ER diagrams, or C4-style views. Keep labels specific and detail proportional to the decision. Validate syntax and ensure the diagram matches the implementation or stated design. Distinguish current state from proposed state. Add text for essential relationships and avoid decorative complexity.

## Completion checks
- State what was inspected and what remains unknown.
- Keep the work within the user's scope and authorization.
- Preserve unrelated behavior and existing project conventions.
- Verify important changes with an appropriate observable check.
- Report evidence, caveats, and unverified areas separately.
