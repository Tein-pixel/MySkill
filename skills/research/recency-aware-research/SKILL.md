# Recency Aware Research

## Workflow

Translate relative time into a concrete date range and state the research snapshot date. Search primary sources, official release notes, repositories, changelogs, and original announcements. Compare publication date with event date. Check whether results meet the requested period and rerun stale searches. Separate current facts from background, forecasts, and social sentiment. Cite claims and disclose source gaps.

## Completion checks
- State what was inspected and what remains unknown.
- Keep the work within the user's scope and authorization.
- Preserve unrelated behavior and existing project conventions.
- Verify important changes with an appropriate observable check.
- Report evidence, caveats, and unverified areas separately.
