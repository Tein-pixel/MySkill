# Academic Writing Review

## Workflow

Clarify discipline, assignment or publication type, citation style, word limit, and source requirements. Build a question and outline before drafting. Cite verifiable sources close to the claim. Never fabricate papers, DOI values, quotations, data, methods, or page numbers. Preserve the author's meaning. Check whether evidence supports claim strength, disclose limitations, and flag unsupported passages.

## Completion checks
- State what was inspected and what remains unknown.
- Keep the work within the user's scope and authorization.
- Preserve unrelated behavior and existing project conventions.
- Verify important changes with an appropriate observable check.
- Report evidence, caveats, and unverified areas separately.
