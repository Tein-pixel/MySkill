# Conversion Rate Optimization

## Workflow

Define conversion event, audience, traffic source, funnel, baseline, and constraints. Inspect analytics quality before interpreting drop-offs. Combine session evidence, user research, usability, and performance to form testable hypotheses. Prioritize experiments by impact, confidence, and effort. Avoid changing multiple variables without sound design. Use truthful messaging and consent. Report sample size, duration, uncertainty, and guardrail metrics rather than declaring a winner from noise.

## Completion checks
- State what was inspected and what remains unknown.
- Keep the work within the user's scope and authorization.
- Preserve unrelated behavior and existing project conventions.
- Verify important changes with an appropriate observable check.
- Report evidence, caveats, and unverified areas separately.
