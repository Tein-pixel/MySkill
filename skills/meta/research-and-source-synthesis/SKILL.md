# Research And Source Synthesis

Define question, scope, freshness, and decision. Prefer official docs, standards, original repositories, papers, and release notes. Verify dates and versions. Cross-check important claims. Separate facts, source claims, inference, and unknowns. Cite claims and disclose research limits.

## Completion checks
- Confirm the task matches this skill's scope.
- Inspect relevant source, project, and user constraints.
- State assumptions and unknowns instead of inventing facts.
- Prefer small, reversible steps and preserve existing work.
- Verify with evidence appropriate to the task.
- Report what was verified, what was not run, and remaining risks.
